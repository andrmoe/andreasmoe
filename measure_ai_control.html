Measuring AI Control

Written in October 2026

  1. Capability of the agent = a
  2. Capability of the monitor = m
  3. Performance loss = l (pegged at 5%)
  4. Attack success rate = r (pegged at 5%)
  5. Monitor usage / Agent usage = u (pegged at 1)
  6. Human control cost per action = h

I want to figure out how fast the capability ceiling for controllable models is increasing. This decomposes into two parts. One is how fast the capability of trustable models is increasing. The other is how fast the capability ratio between the trusted models and the resulting controllable untrusted model is increasing. Cc = Ct * R = Ct0 * exp(Tc * t) * R0 * exp(Tr * t) = Ct * R0 * exp((Tc + Tr) * t) So we're interested in the sum of the growth rates.
