My opinions on technical AI safety priorities

Written in October 2026

  1. AI control: On track to achieve its goals. Concluded from this investigation by Claude Code.
  2. Pre-deployment evals: Eval thoroughness is not increasing, which it would have to do to keep pace with capabilities. The bottleneck is not on the number or capability of eval orgs, but the time and access given by labs.
  3. Technical compute governance: There's only recently been a prototype hardware design. This is hard to judge, but I don't think that sufficient uptake is possible in time.
  4. Model interpretability: No opinion yet
  5. Scientist AI: I've heard claims from LawZero. I am sceptical of their claims, because I haven't found anything in public that supports them.
  6. Other AI alignment research: No opinion yet
