Model, limitations and sources

The simple fit has one shared capability-ratio slope and one strength per protocol. It has no time trend, study offsets, model-specific slopes or metric offsets. All usable observations participate in fitting. Protocols tested at only one ratio borrow the common scaling response rather than establish their own.

Only records with placed attacker and monitor capability estimates enter the ratio fit. Human attacks, unmeasured capability placeholders and baselines without trusted review remain in the archive. Treating repeated observations as conditionally independent can make the 90% credible intervals optimistic.

The unified failure proxy assumes that different outcome definitions share a ratio response after accounting for protocol strength. It does not establish that a trusted model cannot cause harm, or remove cross-benchmark differences. Read the timeline together with the scatterplot's residual spread.
